Aakash Kumar Singh

aakashkumar@iisc.ac.in

I am a Master's Research student at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, where I work in the Video Analytics Lab (VAL), advised by Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu.

I focus on post-training methods designed to make generative AI safe, reliable, and controllable. My work addresses critical challenges in the field, ranging from safety and privacy via selective forgetting methods, to physical plausibility, where I utilize 3D grounding and object affordance to create robust world models.

I have collaborated on research problems with Adobe and Flipkart. Prior to joining IISc, I completed my B.Tech at Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology (MAIT).
